Problem

Bank drafts require a branch visit

Every bank draft request requires teller involvement at the branch. This makes the process depend on staff being available in person. It also takes more time for customers to complete their request.

45min
Estimated service time

Research

Understanding the service experience

In collaboration with the user research team, support call data analysis showed a 15–45 min wait and 10–15 min processing time.

Usability testing in Maze of an initial prototype flow revealed an insight: uncertainty around what happens after submission.

Voice-of-customer feedback

Do I just come back later to see if it’s ready, or will I get a notification when it’s done?

Initial prototype flow

Add request details → Select pickup branch → Confirm submission → Status tracking

Solution

Leveraging existing Request Centre

In collaboration with the PM, we extended the existing Request Centre, building on a familiar mental model and reducing development effort.

TRADE OFF
Required cross-team coordination but avoided duplicating system infrastructure.

Iteration

Improving location selection

The key decision was to use a branch selector due to the constraint that physical bank drafts must be picked up in person.

BEFORE
All branch hours were shown at once, making locations difficult to compare.
AFTER
Branch hours were revealed progressively, making comparison easier.
